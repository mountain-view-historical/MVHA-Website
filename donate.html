Donate

Thanks to the generosity of our community, the Association has preserved priceless artifacts, provided free historical lectures, published a comprehensive history of Mountain View, and installed interpretive plaques and signs throughout the city.

Ways to give

Option one

Give online

The quickest way to support our work. Pay by card through Stripe, our payment provider, and your receipt arrives by email straight away.

Make a donation

Option two

Give by post

Send your name and address with a check payable to The Mountain View Pioneer & Historical Association:

P.O. Box 252
Mountain View, CA 94042

Option three

Add to our archive

Donate photographs, documents or artifacts of local historical significance. Fill in the item donation form and email it to us.

Item donation form (PDF)

We are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, EIN 94-6115407. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Online donation

Make a gift

Choose an amount below and we will hand you over to Stripe, the company that handles our card payments. Your card details are typed on Stripe’s own secure page — never on this website, and never by email — and a receipt is emailed to you as soon as the payment goes through.

You can change the amount, and say what the gift is for, on Stripe’s page before you pay.

Give a little every month

Online giving is being set up. In the meantime please send a check payable to The Mountain View Pioneer & Historical Association to P.O. Box 252, Mountain View, CA 94042, or email info@mountainviewhistorical.org and we will help.

Named fund

Castro Street MVHS monument

To give online, choose Castro Street MVHS monument where Stripe asks what your gift is for.

To give by post, make your check payable to The Mountain View Pioneer & Historical Association and write MVHS monument in the memo line.

Mail to: Mountain View Historical Association, P.O. Box 252, Mountain View, CA 94042

About the monument

Mountain View Historical Association

Founded in 1954, the MVHA is a non-profit, volunteer-run organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich history of Mountain View, California.

MVHA is a registered 501(c)(3). EIN 94-6115407.
