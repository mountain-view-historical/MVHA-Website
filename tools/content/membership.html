Joining online

Choosing a membership above takes you to Stripe, the company that handles our card payments. Your card details are typed on Stripe’s own secure page — never on this website, and never by email — and a receipt is emailed to you the moment the payment goes through.

Joining online is being set up. In the meantime please send your name and address with a check payable to the Mountain View Historical Association to P.O. Box 252, Mountain View, CA 94042, or email membership@mountainviewhistorical.org and we will help.

What Stripe asks you

  • Your name, email address and postal address
  • Whether this is a new membership or a renewal
  • Which newsletter you would like — digital, print, both or none
  • Anyone else in your household the membership should cover

That is everything the Membership Committee needs. It arrives with the payment, so nothing has to be sent on separately.

Two ways to pay for a household membership, whichever suits you better:

  • Join for a year — a single $30 payment. We will write to you when it is time to renew.
  • Join and renew automatically — $30 now and $30 each year afterwards, until you tell us to stop. You can cancel at any time by emailing membership@mountainviewhistorical.org.

If you would like to help as well as join, have a look at the volunteer opportunities and tell us which appeal — there is always something that needs doing, and no experience is required.
