The Mountain ReView

Our quarterly newsletter, featuring articles on the history of Mountain View and updates on the Association’s events and activities.

As part of our mission to share Mountain View’s history with the community, digital versions of the newsletter are made available to the public free of charge.

Members may request a printed copy delivered by post. Members are also welcome to submit original stories and photographs of local historical interest for publication.

About the archive

Every issue we have, back to 1995

The list above runs from February 1995 to the latest issue, and every issue in it can be searched. A few issues are still missing, most of them from 2008 and 2009. Issues from 2005 to 2008 and from 2012 to 2014 survive only as black-and-white scans.

If you have a copy of a missing issue, or a colour original of one of the black-and-white scans, please tell us. We would love to add it.

Looking for something specific?

The Mountain View History Center holds a complete paper run of the newsletter, along with the Association’s wider archive of scrapbooks, yearbooks, diaries, photographs and maps.

A volunteer librarian is there on Tuesday afternoons and can help you find what you are after.
