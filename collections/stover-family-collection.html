Stover Family Collection

1 box · 1870-196X

About this collection

Significance
James T. Stover was born in Maine in 1847. Married Ella E Hunt in 1870 in Maine. Found in 1910 census in Mountain View. Died in 1934.
Scope and contents
Bible, Photographs, Portraits, Newspapers, Newsletters
Subjects
Stover Family, Mountain View Register-Leader, San Jose Municipal Records, Chester Awalt, Castro Street, Mountain View Businesses
Creator
various
Dates
1870-196X
Place
Mountain View and San Jose
Extent
1 box
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.5.2
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Mr. & Mrs. Clifford O. Hall (from Susie Stover estate)
Restrictions on access
None
