McPheeters Family Archives

1 box · 1884-1975

About this collection

Significance
John Luther McPheeters was born in Missouri and married Ada Elizabeth Swall. Long time Mountain View family.
Scope and contents
books, newspaper clippings, blueprints, house specs, photos, deeds
Subjects
school books, property deeds
Creator
various
Dates
1884-1975
Place
Mountain View
Extent

Language

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.
