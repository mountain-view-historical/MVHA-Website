McCarty Family Archives

2 boxes · 1872-1962

About this collection

Significance
Public School Registers from 1890-1904, Personal letters and effects belonging to Eldora Freeman McCarty, Deeds and trusts passed to McCarty family
Scope and contents
Graduation Certificates, Mountain View Public School Registers (date range 1890-1904), scrapbook, (1) rock, (1) brick, brown filing box with letters, Scrapbooks, (8) Manila envelopes (contents include photographs, land deeds, wills, artwork, songbook), pin, photo albums, loose photographs, Guitar music book
Subjects
Luke McCarty, Eldora Freeman McCarty, Jack McCarty, Mountain View Public School, Mountain View Grammar School; wills; deeds
Creator
various
Dates
1872-1962
Place
Mountain View
Extent
2 boxes
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.3.5
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Restrictions on access
None
