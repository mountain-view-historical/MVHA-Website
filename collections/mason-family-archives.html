Mason Family Archives

1 box · 1919-1957

About this collection

Significance
Personal effects belonging to Lloyd Mason (fireman / police officer) as well as historical records surrounding news and shop openings in Mountain View, CA.
Scope and contents
Award ribbons, police and fire department badges, newspaper clippings, print
Subjects
Mountain View Fire Department, Mountain View Police, Annual Prune and Apricot Exposition, 1957 Jet Crash, My Dolly’s Hospital, People: Ina Mason
Creator
various
Dates
1919-1957
Place
Mountain View
Extent
1 box
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.3.5
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Dietta Rutledge
