McKenzie Family-2 Archives

1 box · 1907-1935

About this collection

Significance
The McKenzie family papers from the early 1900s. In 1888, Michael McKenzie headed west from Vermont. In 1896, Michael married Mary Byrne and purchased 10 acres of orchard land in Loyola Corners. Michael planted his land with apricots, prunes, pears, walnuts, and apples. They had four children: John Rosario, Bridget, Ellen, and Robert. McKenzie Park in Los Altos is named after the family.
