Dale Family Archives

4 boxes · 1864-1976

About this collection

Significance
The Dale family was an early MV pioneer family.
Scope and contents
books, newspaper clippings, magazines
Subjects
bibles, school books, property deeds
Creator
various
Dates
1864-1976
Place
Mountain View
Extent
4 boxes
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.2.3, B.2.3, B.2.4, B.2.4
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Eleanor Dale
Restrictions on access
None

13 items in 4 boxes or groups, listed as the volunteers found them.
