Winters Collection

2 boxes · 1850s-1970s

About this collection

Significance
The files put together from the Mildred Winters Collection. Mildred Gentry Winters was a professor at San Jose State University in the History Department for 40 years.
Scope and contents
research notes, newspaper clippings, presentation scripts
Subjects
Mountain View History
Creator
various
Dates
1850s-1970s
Place
Mountain View
Extent
2 boxes
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room A.1.4
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Mrs Mildred Winters (1907-1994)
Accession number
2025-xx-xxx
Restrictions on access
None
