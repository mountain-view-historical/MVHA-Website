Weaver Photography Archives

3 boxes · 1986-2002

About this collection

Significance
Bob Weaver took photographs for Mountain View City events like park dedications, city council portraits, etc.
Scope and contents
Mountain View city events, city park dedications, city council
Subjects
photographs, slides, negatives
Creator
various
Dates
1986-2002
Place
Mountain View
Extent
3 boxes
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
History Center Auxilary Room
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Mr. Robert Weaver

Restrictions on access
