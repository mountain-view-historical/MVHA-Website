Mockbee Family Archives

1 box · 1893-1995

About this collection

Significance
The Mockbee family was an early MV pioneer family.
Scope and contents
photos, newspapers, clippings, memorabilia
Subjects
Mockbee, Castro Street; School; Mountain View High School
Creator
various
Dates
1893-1995
Place
Mountain View
Extent
1 box
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept

Owned by
