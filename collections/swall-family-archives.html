Swall Family Archives

4 boxes · 1850-1972

About this collection

Significance
The Swall family was an early MV family.
Scope and contents
books, photos, documents, personal items, billy club, postcards
Subjects
bibles, photo albums, photographs, family portraits, Mountain View History, Swall Family, Rogers Family, Caldwell Family
Creator
various
Dates
1850-1972
Place
Mountain View and various US locations
Extent
4 boxes
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.5.3 (box 1), B.5.3 (box 2), B.5.4 (box 3), B.5.4 (box 4)
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Marion Durkee; Possible Swall family decendants
Accession number
2025-xx-xxx
Restrictions on access
None
