Manfredi Family Archives

4 boxes · 1880-198x

About this collection

Significance
Victor Manfredi came to the United States from Italy in 1880. He opened this grocery store in "Old Mountain View" (near present-day El Calmino Real at Grant Road) shortly after his arrival. The family was active in MV development for many years. Other Manfredi family include Andrew Manfredi, Anna Manfredi, Lucy Manfredi, John Manfredi.

business ledgers, notary books, yearbooks, photos, property deeds

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.
