Semino Family Archives

1 box · 1869-1988

About this collection

Significance
The Semino family was an early MV pioneer family.
Scope and contents
Ledgers; fire insurance policies; newspaper clippings; memoirs; law documents; receipts; deeds; mortgage documents
Subjects
Semino Family; Fruit; Insurance; Land Deeds
Creator
various
Dates
1869-1988
Place
Mountain View
Extent
1 box
Language
English; Italian

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.4.5
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Restrictions on access
None
