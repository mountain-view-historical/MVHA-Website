MVHS Artifacts

2 boxes · 1924-1986

About this collection

Significance
The Mountain View High School first campus was built in 1902, second campus 1924, and Awalt High School was renamed MVHS in 1982
Scope and contents
high school newspapers, football programs, photos
Subjects
MVHS; high school, pieces of 1924 campus
Creator

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.
