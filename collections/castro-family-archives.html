Castro Family Archives

1 box · 1856-1930

About this collection

Significance
The Castros are one of Mountain View's founding families. Much of present day Mountain View and Sunnyvale sits on former Castro family land.
Scope and contents
Photos, books, papers, eye glasses
Subjects
Deeds, will, certificates, legal documents, school papers, family photos, prayer books
Creator
various
Dates
1856-1930
Place
Santa Clara County
Extent
1 box
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
