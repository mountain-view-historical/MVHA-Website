Taylor Family Archives

1 box · 1881-1969

About this collection

Significance
Samuel Taylor born 1824. He worked in merchandising, farming and butchering. He served for ten years as the Village Postmaster and as an agent for Wells, Fargo & Co. Express.
Scope and contents
photo books, newspaper clippings
Subjects
school journal, photo album, city directory

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.
