Cutter Family Archives

1 box · 1852-1953

About this collection

Significance
Cutter family lived in Mountain View in the early 1900s and A.B. Cutter served on the city council in 1932.
Scope and contents
printed books, letters, photos
Subjects
school books; bibles; book of psalms; letters WWI; newspaper clippings; family photos
Creator

Place

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.
