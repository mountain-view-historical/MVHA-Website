Mountain View Woman's Club

4 boxes · 1906-1959

About this collection

Significance
MV Woman's Club papers
Scope and contents
minutebooks, scrapbooks, handbooks, loose papers (newspaper clippings, letters, photographs) pertaining to Mountain View Woman’s Club
Subjects
Women; Woman’s History; Society; Everyday Life; Mountain View Woman’s Club; Mountain View Home Extension Group
Creator
various
Dates
1906-1959
Place
Mountain View; Loma Prieta District 9
Extent
4 boxes
Language
English

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept
Storage room B.4.4, B.4.5, B.4.5
Owned by
Mountain View Historical Association
Given by
Diane Keenan
Restrictions on access
None
