Fire Department archives

About this collection

Significance
Fire Deparment is a critical public service
Scope and contents
Books, newspaper clippings, correspondence, photos, business records, fire records,
Subjects
fire department, fires
Creator
Mountain View Fire Deparment

1905-1978
Place

Seeing it in person

This collection is kept at the Mountain View Public Library and is not on open shelves. Ask the librarian at the History Center to bring it out, and give them the location and box number below — it saves them a search.

Where it is kept

Owned by
