Klein Family ArchivesThe Klein family were winemakers in the early 1900s and won several medals. Pierre Klein (1855-1922) was an Alsatian who came to California in 1875. For years, as manager of the restaurant in San Francisco’s Occidental Hotel, he championed the best of California wines. In 1888 he purchased 160 acres on Monte Bello Ridge (currently known as the Jimsomare Ranch.). In the early 1890s, he began selling his Mira Valle wines to several San Francisco restaurants; in 1895, he entered his wine in the Bordeaux Exposition, where he took an honorable mention At the Paris Exposition of 1900, he won two gold medals—one for his Claret, the other for his “Grand Vin”—known as the “Château Lafitte of America,” When phylloxera attacked his vines after the turn of the century, he did not replant. Retiring in 1910, he sold the property in 1913. 2 boxes · 1896-1901 Storage room B.3.1, x.x.x

Seeing the originals

How to ask for a box

The collections are kept in the library’s storage rooms, which are not open to the public. The librarian at the History Center will fetch what you need.

Every guide gives the shelf where the collection is kept and the number of each box. Note down both before you go — with them, the librarian can go straight to the right box.

What a guide covers

Each guide — archivists call them finding aids — describes one donated collection: who gave it, what period it covers, and an item-by-item list of what is in each box.

The guides are written by volunteers and are still growing. Donations kept in the History Center annex are being added as they are inventoried.
